QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Questions HR teams ask us

How large a team can join one session?
10–80 people works well. Above 80 we split into two sittings or add a co-facilitator so the sound still reaches everyone.
Our team is sceptical. Will this feel awkward?
We keep it grounded: no chanting, no forced sharing. It's guided breathing, Tibetan bowls and rest. Sceptics usually leave the calmest.
Is it tied to any religion?
No. It's a secular, nervous-system reset. Nothing devotional, nothing anyone needs to believe in.
How long is a session?
The standard stress reset runs 75 minutes. Half-day and full-day formats are available for offsites and retreats.
On-site, studio or online?
We come to your office anywhere in Gurgaon and Delhi-NCR, host at a partner studio, or run a guided online version for hybrid teams.
What do we need to provide?
A quiet room where people can sit or lie down, and 45 minutes of setup access. We bring bowls, mats and audio.
Is it safe for everyone?
For most people, yes. Anyone who is pregnant or has epilepsy, a pacemaker or a serious medical condition should check with their doctor and tell us beforehand. We share a short screening note with your team in advance.
How is it priced and invoiced?
Quoted per session by team size, location and format, with a fixed number in writing before you commit. We invoice with GST and can work with your PO process.
What notice do you need?
Usually 5–7 days. Rush dates are often possible, so ask.
